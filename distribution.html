How Procurement Works

A practical process for turning a concept into a qualified project quotation.

01

Project Discovery

Share country, site location, product type, intended use, quantity, utilities, dimensions, budget and timeline.

02

Factory Matching

Review available models, drawings, finishes, customization and relevant manufacturer documents.

03

Written Quotation

Confirm retail unit pricing, packaging, lead times, shipping method, additional import and delivery costs, and any quantity discount in a project-specific written quotation.

04

Local Feasibility

Work with qualified professionals to review zoning, structural and energy codes, foundations, permitting, delivery, utility connection and installation.
