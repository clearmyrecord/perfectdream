Direct integration status
Direct API submission and automatic permit issuance are not enabled. Clark County and Pima County use Accela Citizen Access; Las Vegas uses its city dashboard, Phoenix uses SHAPE PHX, Tucson uses TDC Online and Maricopa County uses its Permit Center. Access to agency-supported APIs, test credentials and permission must be established before any direct filing automation.
