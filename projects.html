Project Planning

What every project needs before it becomes real.

Modular products can move faster than traditional construction, but every successful project still needs disciplined planning. Use this checklist before requesting a quote.

01

Project Location

City, state, property type, zoning district, climate, delivery access, site constraints, and nearby utilities all affect project feasibility.

02

Intended Use

Residential, commercial, hospitality, municipal, temporary, emergency, and event uses all trigger different planning requirements.

03

Product Mix

A project may require more than one category: living units, restrooms, pools, offices, storage, cafes, shelters, or utility buildings.

04

Local Compliance

Permits, zoning, engineering, foundation design, fire code, accessibility, inspections, and utility approvals must be handled locally.

05

Logistics & Delivery

Shipping, import, tariffs, delivery route, crane or forklift access, staging, unloading, and site placement must be planned in advance.

06

Budget & Timeline

A clear budget range and realistic timeline help determine whether the project is ready for quote coordination and factory communication.
