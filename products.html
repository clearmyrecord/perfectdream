01 · Container Pools

Factory-built pool concepts for backyards, resorts, clubs, and hospitality projects.

Container pools create a bold, modern alternative to traditional pool construction. They can be explored for residential properties, short-term rentals, resorts, event spaces, clubs, and branded outdoor entertainment areas.

Best for

Backyard pool projects Vacation rental upgrades Resort and glamping amenities Outdoor bars, lounges, and pool clubs Event venues and entertainment spaces

Why buyers explore them

Container pools can offer a modern visual identity, compact footprint, transportable structure, and strong design appeal for high-impact outdoor environments. Site requirements, foundations, utilities, drainage, access, safety barriers, and permits must be confirmed locally.

02 · Expandable & Folding Homes

Flexible modular living units designed for efficient transport and faster deployment.

Expandable and folding homes are designed to compress for shipping and expand into usable space on site. They are useful for buyers who want a practical modular living concept without starting from a traditional ground-up build.

03 · ADUs & Modular Living Units

Backyard housing, guest units, rentals, studios, and compact living concepts.

Modular living units can support accessory dwelling units, backyard guest houses, rental units, office studios, caregiver housing, employee housing, and compact residential concepts. ADU rules vary heavily by state, city, county, zoning district, lot size, utility access, and building code.

Potential applications

Backyard ADUs Guest houses Rental units Home offices and studios Caregiver or family housing Small-lot development concepts

Planning notes

Buyers should confirm zoning, ADU eligibility, utility hookups, setbacks, maximum square footage, foundations, inspections, and local permit requirements before purchasing any modular unit.

06 · Commercial Container Units

Bars, cafes, offices, kitchens, retail pop-ups, and branded business units.

Commercial container builds can create memorable business environments with a strong visual identity. They are useful for entrepreneurs, event operators, developers, commercial landlords, hospitality properties, and brands that want a faster, more flexible physical footprint.

Each commercial unit must be reviewed for local business licensing, food service rules, fire code, electrical service, plumbing, accessibility, signage, occupancy, and permitting requirements.
