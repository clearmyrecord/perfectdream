Project Intake Form

Request modular project information.

This form opens your email app with the project details. For best results, include location, product type, timeline, intended use, and any site constraints.

By submitting, you understand this is an inquiry request. Final pricing, feasibility, engineering, delivery, installation, and permitting requirements must be confirmed before purchase.

What to Include

The more detail you send, the faster we can understand the opportunity.

Modular projects move better when the basic facts are clear from the beginning. Use the checklist below before sending your inquiry.

01

Project Location

Include the city, state, and whether the project is residential, commercial, hospitality, municipal, temporary, or permanent.

02

Product Category

Tell us whether you are exploring an ADU, container pool, restroom/shower unit, commercial container, shelter, resort unit, or custom project.

03

Site Conditions

Share what you know about site access, foundations, utilities, drainage, delivery access, crane/forklift needs, and available space.

04

Budget + Timeline

A realistic budget range and target timeline help determine what product path and project scale make the most sense.

After You Send the Form

What happens next?

We review your inquiry, organize the project details, and help determine whether the opportunity is ready for product matching, quote preparation, and factory communication.

01

Inquiry Review

We review your form for location, use case, product category, budget range, timeline, and project readiness.

02

Clarifying Questions

If details are missing, we may ask for additional information about site conditions, utilities, delivery, local code, and intended use.

03

Product Direction

We help narrow the project toward the most relevant product category or custom modular direction.

04

Quote Preparation

Once the basic scope is clear, we help prepare the information needed for pricing and factory communication.

05

Planning Requirements

Buyers should confirm permits, zoning, engineering, foundations, utilities, installation, inspections, and local code requirements.

06

Next Step

If the project is a fit, we help move the conversation toward product selection, logistics, and purchase coordination.

Important Compliance Notice:

Perfect Dream Modular and United Painters Worldwide, LLC help with project intake, distributor communication, product education, and quote coordination. Permits, zoning, engineering, foundations, utility hookups, installation, tariffs, shipping costs, inspections, and local code compliance vary by jurisdiction and must be confirmed before purchase. Buyers should consult licensed local professionals and permitting authorities.
