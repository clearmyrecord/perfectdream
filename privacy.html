Privacy Notice

Updated November 2026.

When you request a quote or contact us, we receive the contact and project information you provide, which may include your name, email, phone number, project location, type, budget and message. We use it to respond, evaluate potential projects and coordinate with relevant manufacturing and project partners when necessary.

Our forms use FormSend.co to deliver inquiries to our distribution inbox. Your form information is processed by this third party and by email services. Do not send identity documents, payment card information or sensitive personal data through these forms. We do not sell your inquiry information.

Permit preparation packages

Version 4 assembles applicant-entered information, selected files and imported prior reports entirely in your browser. These are not uploaded by the package generator, and no application is filed. The downloaded archive may contain personal or confidential information. Store and share it securely; the user controls subsequent disclosure.

Permit pre-screening

The rules-based permit pre-screen runs in your browser. If you select public parcel lookup, the parcel number and resulting parcel geometry are sent directly to Clark County’s public GIS service, which has its own privacy practices. Optional supporting document selection lists file names and sizes locally only; file contents are not read or uploaded. Downloaded reports remain under your control.

AI architectural drawing review

Version 3 is an owner-only pilot. If you explicitly consent and submit a drawing, the selected file and entered project information are transmitted to our Render-hosted service and OpenAI for preliminary analysis. Upload only files you have permission to share; remove personal details and sensitive information first. Our application processes file contents in memory, does not intentionally save uploads or reports, and does not send drawings to web search tools. API response storage is disabled, but provider security, abuse-monitoring and other retention rules may apply. Standard hosting logs and usage counters may be retained. Reports are not professional design or permit approvals.

AI project planning

The planning questionnaire is processed in your browser until you choose to run AI research or submit an inquiry. If you run research, the project details you entered, including optional parcel identifiers or non-private development site addresses, are transmitted to our hosted planning service and OpenAI for research. Do not provide personal addresses, contact details or sensitive information in the planner. Search queries may be sent to web search providers. Our AI API requests disable response storage, but provider security and abuse-monitoring retention may still apply. Our quota system retains short-lived request counters, one-time verification identifiers and a keyed hash derived from the network address; it does not store project report content. The hosting provider may retain standard access and operational logs. Reports are preliminary and do not establish parcel approval.

You can request access, correction or deletion by emailing Matt@UnitedPaintersWW.com. External service providers and suppliers may have separate privacy practices.
